Why a website

Facebook and WhatsApp get you noticed.
A website helps customers trust you.

Before they get in touch, customers often want to check what you do, see real photos of your work and find out how to reach you. A clear website answers those questions in one place, on a phone, at any hour.

Social media only

  • The algorithm decideswho sees your posts.
  • Posts get buriedwithin a day or two.
  • Rules can change overnightand accounts can be restricted.
  • Little room for detailslike services, areas and prices.

With your own website

  • You own itNot controlled by algorithms or platform changes.
  • Builds trustA professional website makes your business look established.
  • Get found on GoogleWhen people search for services like yours in their area.
  • Everything in one placeYour services, photos, areas covered and prices.
  • Works with social mediaLink your Facebook and Instagram posts back to it.
  • One tap to WhatsApp youVisitors become real enquiries.

Is this right for your business?

A good fit if…

  • You run a service business, such as renovation, aircond servicing, tuition, a clinic, a salon or a workshop.
  • Customers ask you the same questions on WhatsApp again and again.
  • You only have a Facebook page, or your current website is outdated or hard to use on a phone.
  • You want to look more established when customers compare you with others.

Probably not the right fit if…

  • Your Facebook page and WhatsApp Business already bring in all the work you can handle.
  • You need a large online shop with hundreds of products.
  • You are looking for the cheapest website possible.

Not every business needs a website. If yours doesn't, I'll tell you honestly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about pricing, timing and how I work.

How do I get started?

Send me a WhatsApp message at 010-264 1824, or use the form below. I reply within 1 working day, we have a short chat, and then I send you a written quote. There's no obligation.

How much does a website cost?

RM1,600 for a professional, mobile-friendly website with up to 8 pages or sections in one language, including a website chatbot, or RM1,500 without the chatbot. Google Business Profile setup and a standard domain for the first year are included free. Need more pages? Every 2 extra pages or sections is one add-on. Add-ons are RM500 each on their own, or RM400 each when bundled with the website or another add-on.

How long does it take?

Usually 3–5 working days, depending on what your project needs and how quickly you can send your content (text, photos and logo). Each add-on adds 1 working day.

Can you rush my website?

Yes. Rush delivery cuts 2 working days off your delivery time, for example from 3–5 working days to 1–3. It costs an extra 20% of the project total (website plus any add-ons).

Do I need to provide the content?

Yes. You send me your services, contact details, the areas you cover and any photos or logo you have, and I turn it into a clear, professional website. I can help you tidy up the wording.

How do I pay?

By DuitNow, Touch 'n Go eWallet or cash. As I work as a freelancer, payments go to me personally. You pay a RM200 deposit to start and the rest when your website is ready, before launch.

Which languages can my website be in?

Your website includes one language: English, Bahasa Melayu or Chinese. Each extra language is an add-on at RM500, or RM400 when bundled.

Is the domain really free?

Yes, for the first year, for a standard .com, .com.my or .my domain. Premium domains are quoted separately. From the second year, you pay the renewal yourself, unless you're on Website Care, which includes it.

Is hosting included?

For the standard Business Website, hosting is included with optional Website Care (RM128/month); otherwise you pay the hosting provider directly. Restaurant subscriptions include hosting for the initial 24 months. One-time restaurant projects include the first year.

What is Website Care?

Optional Website Care at RM128/month includes hosting, standard domain renewal, technical upkeep, covered fault repairs and 1 small content-update batch per month. New features and larger changes are quoted separately.

What is the hourly rate if I need changes?

For changes outside your plan, labour is RM49/hour for websites built by us, or RM89/hour for websites built elsewhere. We confirm the scope and estimated charge before you approve the work. Included content updates use your plan’s allowance; repairs to covered technical faults during an active support subscription have no additional labour charge.

Can we meet in person?

Yes, if you're in Johor Bahru. Otherwise we can talk by WhatsApp, Zoom or Google Meet.

I already have a website or a Facebook page. Do I need a new one?

Not always. If what you have already works well for your business, I'll tell you. If your website is outdated or hard to use on a phone, I can replace it and reuse what still works.

Who will I be working with?

With me, Adam. Ace Digital Solutions is run by me as a freelance web designer in Johor Bahru, so you deal with the same person from your first message to launch.
