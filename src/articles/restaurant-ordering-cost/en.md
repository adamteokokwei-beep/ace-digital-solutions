A lower commission does not automatically mean a more profitable order. For a Malaysian café or restaurant, the useful question is: **what does it cost to serve the same customer, with the same basket and the same delivery arrangement, through each channel?**

Delivery apps can help people discover your restaurant. A direct ordering website may suit customers who already know you. Both can have a role. This guide compares costs rather than promising more sales or recommending that every restaurant leave delivery platforms.

## A worked example before you choose
The following is an **illustration, not a quotation from GrabFood or foodpanda, a restaurant case study or a savings guarantee**. Assume the same RM50 basket moves from an app to a direct website. Food, packaging and other costs that remain identical are excluded from both sides.

| Assumption | Illustrative amount |
|---|---|
| Restaurant-paid platform charges, including any applicable tax | 20% of order value |
| Website setup spread over a 24-month comparison | RM1,688 ÷ 24 |
| Website subscription | RM168/month |
| Direct payment fee, including assumed 8% tax on that fee | RM1.25 × 1.08 = RM1.35/order |
| Delivery cost remaining with the restaurant after customer contributions | RM3/order |
| Additional direct-channel staff handling and customer acquisition | RM1.50/order |

Here, platform charges are RM10 per order. Direct variable costs are RM5.85: RM1.35 + RM3 + RM1.50. The difference is RM4.15 per comparable order. Direct fixed costs average RM238.33/month: RM168 + RM1,688 ÷ 24. Other fixed provider costs are assumed to be zero in this example, not in every project.

| Comparable orders/month | Platform charges | Direct costs, including spread setup | Direct cost advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | RM500.00 | RM530.83 | −RM30.83 |
| 58 | RM580.00 | RM577.63 | RM2.37 |
| 100 | RM1,000.00 | RM823.33 | RM176.67 |
| 200 | RM2,000.00 | RM1,408.33 | RM591.67 |

**The illustrated cost crossover is 58 comparable orders per month.** It is not a profit forecast. It assumes the customers actually order directly, comparable service and basket values, and the stated costs throughout the 24-month comparison. A higher rider bill, discounts or staff workload can change the answer.

## What are GrabFood and foodpanda merchant fees in Malaysia?
Do not borrow one percentage from another restaurant and treat it as your own rate. [Grab’s merchant page](https://www.grab.com/my/merchant/food/) confirms service fees and describes its ordering and delivery offering. [Foodpanda’s registration FAQ](https://partner.foodpanda.my/en) says the chosen commission package determines the exact rate shown during registration.

Use your agreement and recent settlement statements. Include the charges you actually bear: commission, tax on fees, paid promotions, restaurant-funded discounts and any fixed subscription. Keep consumer-paid delivery charges separate from restaurant expenses. The 20% in our example is an editable scenario, not a published standard for either platform.

Platform discovery, rider coordination and support have value. A direct website does not automatically replace those services. If app orders would disappear rather than shift to your website, a simple fee comparison will overstate the benefit.

## Why “payment fees are only 1–2%” can mislead
At the time of this review, [Billplz’s pricing page](https://main.billplz.com/pricing) lists Basic-plan consumer FPX at RM1.25 per payment and wallet payments at 1.5%. These are different methods, not fees to automatically add together. Billplz’s [pricing guide](https://support.billplz.com/guide/pricing) explains that 8% SST applies to its transaction fees. Activation, plan and method conditions still need checking before choosing a provider.

| Basket value | RM1.25 fixed fee, before tax | RM1.35 fee, with assumed 8% fee tax |
|---|---|---|
| RM30 | 4.17% | 4.50% |
| RM50 | 2.50% | 2.70% |
| RM150 | 0.83% | 0.90% |

The general formula is: **effective payment fee % = ((fixed fee + basket value × percentage rate) × (1 + applicable fee-tax rate) ÷ basket value) × 100**. Enter percentage rates as decimals when calculating by hand. Use only charges applicable to your payment method, and apply each tax to its correct taxable base. The calculator assumes one tax rate on the gateway fee; adjust the inputs or calculate separately if your arrangement differs.

A provider’s advertised lowest rate may require a paid plan. Include that plan’s cost as well as any additional software or integration charges. Recheck the linked provider pages before making a decision.

## The break-even formula—and when there is no crossover
For this calculator, **monthly direct fixed costs = website subscription + other direct fixed costs + setup cost ÷ comparison months**. **Monthly platform cost = comparable orders × platform cost per order + platform fixed costs**.

Subtract direct variable cost per order from platform variable cost per order. If the result is positive, divide the additional direct monthly fixed cost by that saving and round up to a whole order. If the direct channel also has higher variable costs and higher fixed costs, increasing volume will not make it cheaper under those assumptions. Equal variable costs mean only fixed costs determine the difference. The calculator states when neither a normal break-even threshold nor a blanket “cheaper above this volume” conclusion applies.

This spread-cost comparison is **not cash payback**. Setup is paid on the schedule in your quote. The cash example below assumes the full RM1,688 is paid at the outset. At 100 orders/month in the example, RM415 of variable-cost difference minus RM168 subscription leaves RM247/month before other incremental fixed costs. Recovering RM1,688 would take about 7 months at that steady volume. In the first month, direct cash outlay is RM2,441, not RM823.33. Changing demand or costs changes the payback period.

## Pickup and delivery need separate comparisons
- Compare pickup with pickup, and delivery with delivery. A pickup website order has no rider expense; that does not make it equivalent to a platform-delivered order.
- For third-party riders, obtain current quotes for the restaurant’s location, distance, hours and service requirements. Coverage, availability and charges vary. No provider connection or delivery speed is assumed here.
- Count only the delivery cost borne by the restaurant after customer contributions, plus redelivery, cancellations and refunds where applicable.
- Include extra staff time for order acceptance, dispatch, customer questions and reconciliation. Ask who handles a failed payment or missed rider collection.
- Check POS menu, sales and refund support before buying. API access and provider approval are not universal, and custom integration can add setup and ongoing costs.

## When an ordering website may not be the next step
A direct channel may be premature if you have few repeat customers, cannot manage incoming orders reliably or need expensive integration for a small order volume. A clear menu, accurate opening hours and a straightforward enquiry or reservation request form may address the immediate problem first.

A practical sequence is to keep useful discovery channels, make direct ordering convenient for willing repeat customers, and review real contribution after a pilot. Do not treat a customer email list or a new website as proof that orders will move automatically.

## Questions restaurant owners ask
### Is a direct ordering website always cheaper?
No. It depends on comparable order volume, fixed costs, payment fees, delivery and staff or acquisition costs. A lower headline commission is not enough.

### Should I stop using delivery apps?
Not on the basis of this calculation alone. Discovery, convenience and customer preference matter. Compare channel contributions and actual customer behaviour before changing your mix.

### Does this calculator include all taxes and refunds?
No. It has an editable gateway-fee tax assumption and an editable effective platform charge. Use contract-specific figures and add other costs. It does not calculate your business taxes, refund liability or accounting profit.

### Will the calculator store my sales figures?
No. This calculator runs in the browser, does not save its inputs and does not submit them. Avoid entering private account or customer information; it only needs cost assumptions.
