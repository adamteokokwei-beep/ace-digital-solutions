# Internal service operations — proposed launch scope

Not customer marketing copy. This document does not configure logging, alerts or any live infrastructure. Confirm provider visibility, retention, alert delivery and support hours before taking responsibility for a customer system.

## Pricing and scope

Business Website RM888: up to 8 standard pages, one language, free standard domain for the first year, one round of minor revisions. Café/restaurant/stall version: standard design, online ordering, delivery setup after a compatibility check and payment-gateway setup; rider and gateway fees paid separately. Website add-ons: extra page +RM88; language, AI website assistant, online catalogue, advanced local SEO, GEO optimisation from RM388; appointment booking from RM488; payment checkout from RM588; blog +RM288; seasonal theme from RM188; custom form +RM188.

Simple Automation RM388–RM888 (one workflow, 5–7 working days). Accounting Automation RM1,888–RM5,888 (one workflow, 7–12 working days; not bookkeeping, tax advice or an accuracy guarantee; e-Invoice compliance assessed separately). Software/API integration, AI enquiry processing and changes to an existing POS are quoted after a free review.

Care: website upkeep RM88 every 3 months (offered each quarter; without it fixes are RM49/hour); Automation Care RM88/month; Website SEO/GEO Care from RM188/month. Website Care RM128/month is closed to new clients; existing Care clients keep their terms. Security fixes for ADS-managed software never depend on a marketing plan.

## Internal reviews

Care and Essential: automated alerting with human investigation triggered by alerts, anomalies or customer reports; no periodic human security-log review promised.
Signature options: automated alerting plus planned human reviews Monday and Thursday, including messaging/API events for WhatsApp. Investigate critical alerts without waiting for scheduled review, during agreed support hours. No 24-hour staffed monitoring promise.
Automation must be configured and tested per deployment before claiming it is active. Do not log tokens, passwords, full payment details or unnecessary message bodies. Set retention and access for the actual provider and customer risk.

SEO: monthly for Care and Signature. GEO: quarterly for Care, monthly for Signature. Essential excludes SEO/GEO. Proposed internal review size: up to five priority pages; five agreed customer questions across two AI-search services for GEO; a short report per review. Small fixes use plan update allowance, broader writing/development quoted separately. No ranking or AI-inclusion guarantees.

## Contract dependencies

Quote-supported POS/payment/delivery integrations after checking API rights. Custom integrations separately priced. Provider/rider fees and agreed AI/WhatsApp allowances separate from unlimited-service promises. Refunds require authorised staff approval.
Delivery: Essential 7–9 days; Signature 14–16 days after content/access are received, subject to agreed integration dependencies.
Hosting: first 24 months of subscription. Domain, renewal, handover, support hours/response targets and cancellation terms must be documented in the quote. Owner requested three payment warnings four days apart; precise suspension timing still requires contract confirmation. No invented termination fee.
Shared out-of-scope rates: RM49/hour for sites we built, RM89/hour for externally built sites, after scope/estimate approval. Covered faults during active subscription incur no extra labour charge.

Rush delivery (website and automation projects only): +25% of the project total for about 20% fewer working days, rounded down (5–7 → 4–5; 7–12 → 5–9). POS plans have no rush option.
