# Internal service operations — proposed launch scope

Not customer marketing copy. This document does not configure logging, alerts or any live infrastructure. Confirm provider visibility, retention, alert delivery and support hours before taking responsibility for a customer system.

## Pricing and scope

Business Website RM1,600; optional Care RM128/month. Care includes hosting, standard domain renewal, covered technical fault repairs and one small existing-text/image update batch monthly.
Restaurant Essentials RM1,688 + RM168/month, minimum 24 months. Signature RM3,288 + RM368/month. Signature + WhatsApp RM3,888 + RM468/month. Initial-term totals RM5,720/RM12,120/RM15,120.
Signature options include two consolidated menu-update requests monthly for existing names/prices/photos/availability during the 24-month subscription and up to eight agreed seasonal refreshes annually. No unlimited updates or new functionality.

## Internal reviews

Care and Essentials: automated alerting with human investigation triggered by alerts, anomalies or customer reports; no periodic human security-log review promised.
Signature options: automated alerting plus planned human reviews Monday and Thursday, including messaging/API events for WhatsApp. Investigate critical alerts without waiting for scheduled review, during agreed support hours. No 24-hour staffed monitoring promise.
Automation must be configured and tested per deployment before claiming it is active. Do not log tokens, passwords, full payment details or unnecessary message bodies. Set retention and access for the actual provider and customer risk.

SEO: monthly for all subscriptions. GEO: quarterly for Care/Essentials, monthly for Signature options. Proposed internal review size: up to five priority pages; five agreed customer questions across two AI-search services for GEO; a short report per review. Small fixes use plan update allowance, broader writing/development quoted separately. No ranking or AI-inclusion guarantees.

## Contract dependencies

Quote-supported POS/payment/delivery integrations after checking API rights. Custom integrations separately priced. Provider/rider fees and agreed AI/WhatsApp allowances separate from unlimited-service promises. Refunds require authorised staff approval.
Delivery: Essentials 5–7 days; Signature options 12–14 days after content/access are received, subject to agreed integration dependencies.
Hosting: first 24 months of subscription; first year of one-time restaurant projects. Domain, renewal, handover, support hours/response targets and cancellation terms must be documented in the quote. Owner requested three payment warnings four days apart; precise suspension timing still requires contract confirmation. No invented termination fee.
Shared out-of-scope rates: RM49/hour for sites we built, RM89/hour for externally built sites, after scope/estimate approval. Covered faults during active subscription incur no extra labour charge.
