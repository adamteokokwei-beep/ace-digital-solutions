How do I get started?

Book a free consultation using the form. We'll talk through your business, the look you want, the pages you need and any add-ons, then send you a clear quote. There's no obligation.

How much does it cost?

$250 one-time for a professional, mobile-friendly website with up to 5 pages or sections, including free Google Business Profile setup. Optional add-ons are $150 on their own, or just $100 each with your website. Website Care is optional at $49/month and includes hosting.

How long does it take?

Typical delivery is 3–5 business days once we have your business details, photos and logo. Every 2 add-ons add 1 business day. Need it sooner? Rush delivery reduces the delivery time by 2 business days for an extra 20% of your project total.

Can you rush my website?

Yes. Rush delivery reduces your delivery time by 2 business days (for example, 3–5 business days becomes 1–3). It costs an extra 20% of your one-time project total (website plus any add-ons).

Do I need to provide the content?

Yes. You provide your services, contact details, service area and any photos or logo you have, and we turn it into a clear, professional website.

Can you use my existing domain?

Yes. Domain connection assistance is included, so we'll help you point your existing domain to the new website.

How do I pay?

All payments are made through PayPal, a secure and widely trusted payment service, so you never share your card or bank details with us directly. You pay 50% to start and 50% when your website is ready.

When is the final payment due?

The final 50% is due once you've reviewed your website and are happy with it. It must be paid before we help you host and launch the website, or before we transfer the website files to you.

Is hosting included?

Hosting is not included in the $250 website. It is included if you choose the Website Care plan ($49/month). Otherwise, you pay your hosting provider directly, and we help you set it up when your website launches.

What if I already have a website?

That's fine. We can replace an outdated website with a new, modern one and use what still works from your current content.

What is Website Care?

An optional $49/month plan that keeps your website running, updated and looked after: hosting included, health monitoring, minor updates, security and maintenance checks, social media update monitoring where supported, and ongoing support.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

All kinds of local and small businesses. Trades and home services like plumbers, electricians, roofers, HVAC, landscapers and contractors are just where we started. We also build for salons and beauty, cleaning, auto, fitness, health and wellness, restaurants and cafés, professional services, churches and ministries, and more. If you serve customers, we can build you a website that brings in enquiries.

Can the chatbot estimate the cost for my website?

Yes. Open the chatbot, pick the add-ons you want, and it shows your total and deposit. You can send that estimate straight into the enquiry form.

What if I need an urgent fix without Website Care?

No problem. If something needs fixing urgently and you're not on the Website Care plan, small fixes and changes are charged at $29 per hour.

My story

Good businesses deserve to look good online.

I started Ace Digital Solutions to give local businesses a professional website at a price that makes sense for a small business. Plumbers, electricians, roofers and other trades do great work, but many are hard to find online, or have a website that no longer reflects the quality of what they do.

Before web design, I was a teacher. Teaching taught me to listen carefully, explain things simply and stay patient. I bring the same habits to every project: I ask about your business, your customers and the jobs you want more of, and I keep you updated in plain English.

The result is a website that looks professional, works well on a phone, and makes it easy for customers to call you or send an enquiry.
