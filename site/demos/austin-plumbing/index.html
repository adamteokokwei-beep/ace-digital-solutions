Find and close your main water valve (most Austin homes):
  1. Look where the water line enters the house: often the garage, a utility closet, or outside near the front hose bib.
  2. Turn the round handle clockwise, or turn a lever handle so it sits across the pipe.
  3. No luck? The meter box near the street has a shut-off too. Then call us and we'll talk you through it.
