Find and close your main water valve:
  1. Landed house: look next to the water meter, usually near the front gate or the boundary wall.
  2. Condo or apartment: check the meter compartment along the corridor, or ask the management office.
  3. Turn the handle clockwise, or turn a lever so it sits across the pipe. Still stuck? Call us and we'll talk you through it.

FAQ

JB plumbing questions, answered

Do you offer emergency plumbing in Johor Bahru?

Yes. We're on call 24 hours, including nights, weekends and public holidays. For burst pipes or major leaks, turn off your main water valve and call us right away.

How quickly can a plumber come?

Most calls in the JB area get a same-day visit. Emergencies are sent out right away, and we'll give you an honest arrival time on the phone.

Do you give prices before starting work?

Yes. Our website shows typical price ranges, and your plumber confirms the exact price on site before any work begins.

Do you charge extra on weekends or public holidays?

No. Same rates every day of the week.

Can I book online?

Yes. Pick your problem, choose a time slot, and leave your name and number. It takes about a minute.

Do you work in condos and apartments?

Yes. We can coordinate with your management office if a work permit or access to the water meter is needed.

How do I pay?

By cash, bank transfer or DuitNow after the job is done.
