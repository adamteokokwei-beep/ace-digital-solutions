FAQ
Questions parents ask.

    

      
How big are the classes?
No more than 8 students per class, so teachers can check every child's work.

      
Which language are classes taught in?
Most classes are in English, with explanations in Bahasa Melayu or Chinese when it helps. Language subjects are taught in that language.

      
What if my child misses a class?
Let us know on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a replacement class in the same month, subject to seats.

      
Do you give homework?
Yes, a little each week, and we help with school homework during primary classes.

      
How do I pay?
Monthly, by DuitNow or cash at the centre, before the first class of the month.
