English

This notice explains how Ace Digital Solutions uses the personal data you share through this website. Ace Digital Solutions is run by Adam as a freelance web designer in Johor Bahru, Malaysia.

1. What I collect

  • From the contact form: your name and WhatsApp number (required), and your business name, what you'd like help with, your message and your email address (optional).
  • From the website chat assistant: the messages you type in the chat.
  • If you contact me on WhatsApp, by email or on Facebook: the details you send me there.

All of this comes directly from you.

2. Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk about your project.
  • To prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, to build and support your website.
  • To keep basic records of enquiries and projects.

I do not sell your personal data, and I do not add you to marketing lists.

3. Who else handles it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the contact form to my email inbox.
  • The chat assistant sends the messages you type to Google's Gemini AI service, through a Cloudflare server, to write its replies. Under Google's terms for this service, Google may use what is typed to improve its products. Please don't type sensitive personal information (such as IC numbers or bank details) into the chat.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages you choose to send me on WhatsApp, including any chat summary you choose to send from the website.
  • Google (Gmail) stores emails I receive. The website itself is hosted on Cloudflare.

These services may process data outside Malaysia.

4. Do you have to give it?

Your name and WhatsApp number are needed so I can reply to you. Everything else is optional. If you don't provide the required details, I won't be able to reply through the form, but you can still WhatsApp or email me directly.

5. How long I keep it

I keep your details only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or project and to keep reasonable business records, and then delete them.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal data I hold about you, ask me to stop using it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Contact me at:

  • WhatsApp: 010-264 1824
  • Email: adamteokokwei@gmail.com
